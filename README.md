## 🔬 Research & Exploration | Recherche & Exploration

### Neuromorphic Computing & Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)

Exploring alternatives to traditional GPU-dependent AI. Investigating brain-inspired computing architectures for ultra-efficient, real-time AI inference — especially for voice-to-voice systems with minimal latency.

Explorer les alternatives à l'IA dépendante des GPU traditionnels. Étudier les architectures de calcul bio-inspirées pour une inférence IA ultra-efficace en temps réel — en particulier pour les systèmes voix-à-voix à latence minimale.

**Hardware under investigation:**
- Intel Loihi — neuromorphic research chip
- BrainChip Akida — commercial neuromorphic processor
- NVIDIA Grace Blackwell — next-gen GPU/CPU architecture
- NPU alternatives — exploring edge AI acceleration

### Energy Systems & Advanced Materials

Conceptualizing and prototyping novel energy conversion and monitoring systems:

- **Thermomagnetic energy conversion** — supercritical CO₂ + graphene oxide systems
- **Ultrasonic monitoring** — repurposed piezoelectric sensors for elevator cable integrity
- **Electromagnetic applications** — innovative approaches to sustainable energy

### Voice AI & Real-Time Inference

Building toward seamless, human-like voice-to-voice AI interaction with sub-100ms latency, running on local hardware. No cloud dependency. No data leaks. Just fast, private, intelligent conversation.
